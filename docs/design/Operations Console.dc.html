1Operations Console — the "manage" phase
Modes. Every desktop frame has a working Cadet / Engineer toggle in its header. Cadet: plain sentences, metaphors, max 3 actions. Engineer: the same panels plus numbers, equations and ⓘ sources.
Timeline of the story. Day 214 cruise → storm (hazard) → Day 215 safe mode → Day 307 orbit insertion → Day 418 queue before conjunction → Day 431 blackout → Day 672 extension decision.
Physics check. A solar conjunction can't happen during an Earth→Mars cruise (Earth and craft stay on the same side of the Sun), so the blackout is placed in the science orbit, when Earth and Mars are on opposite sides. Positions on the map are worked out from orbits, not placed by eye.
Numbers. Placeholders that agree with each other (light delay = distance ÷ c, power ∝ 1/r², Δv from Tsiolkovsky). Real-history boxes are tagged TO VERIFY. Icons are drawn as line icons to match the Build Bay, not emoji.
Live bits. Light-delay pulses move along the Earth–craft line. In 1f pick a response and Send: the countdown runs at 30× and then shows the result. Speed buttons work. Drag the handles on the Power dial in 1e.
